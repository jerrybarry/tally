Trust & Security:

• macOS Keychain: Router passwords are encrypted via Electron’s safeStorage directly in your macOS Keychain and never stored in plain text.
• Lockout Protection: The router firmware locks logins for several minutes after 3 consecutive wrong passwords. Tally automatically stops polling upon a single rejected login to keep your router accessible.
